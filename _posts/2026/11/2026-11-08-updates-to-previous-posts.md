Here are the 4 posts I updated and why:

* **[Too much coffee?](/blog/too-much-coffee):** In April, I wrote about quitting coffee after hitting a point where caffeine no longer seemed to help. About six months later, I went back to drinking coffee, and the [update section](/blog/too-much-coffee#update) explains why.
* **[Buying a car in the age of AI](/blog/buying-a-car-in-age-of-ai):** This September post ended with me buying a 2019 Honda Civic. I ended up returning the Civic and buying a 2026 Subaru Forester Hybrid instead. The [update section](/blog/buying-a-car-in-age-of-ai#update-returning-the-civic) covers how and why.
* **[Barefoot shoes, basketball, and how to avoid recurring calf strains](/blog/barefoot-shoes-basketball-fix-for-recurring-calf-strains):** I added a note at the top of this 2021 post saying that I no longer recommend zero-drop shoes and why.
* **[Documentation theme for Jekyll](/documentation-theme-jekyll/):** [Release 8.0](https://idratherbewriting.com/documentation-theme-jekyll/mydoc_release_notes_80.html) redesigns the theme on Bootstrap 5 so that it looks more like idratherbewriting.com. I know Jekyll is no longer the static site generator of choice, but it continues to work well for me, and its integration with GitHub simplifies continuous publishing for a blog.
